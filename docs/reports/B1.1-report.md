# B1.1 report — plain labels, "Mark finished", closing a budget, extras

Prompt: `docs/prompts/28-filmbill-B1.1-finish-close-extras.md`. Built 2026-11-24/25.

**In plain words.** The budget now speaks plainly: "Forecast final cost",
"Still to spend", "Under / over budget" with the word, an info icon on every
column and a worked example; EFC and ETC are gone from the screen. A line
that is done can be marked finished, so its leftover money shows as profit.
A locked budget can be closed at the production's end after a review of what
does not add up. Extras agreed after the quote can be proposed, accepted or
declined even on a locked budget, and only accepted ones count.

## Defaults I chose

- **"Under plan and not finished"** means *spent* below planned (with the
  default rule a line's forecast is never below its plan, so "forecast below
  plan" would list nothing).
- **"At least one sentence"** = at least three words.
- `clean` = nothing in any of the five lists; otherwise within/over by the
  total forecast against the total plan.
- A closed budget also refuses **voids**, unlocking and line reopen (every
  write in the cost router goes through one gate). [D91, open decision]
- Finish all covers sub-sections' lines; Reopen all reopens only finished
  lines (a typed non-zero still-to-spend stays).
- An extra defaults to the company's **standard** tax category, period
  `flat`. Costs only on accepted extras; an extra with live costs cannot be
  declined; a declined extra cannot be edited.
- **The production fee applies to extras** (they are ordinary lines in the
  document). Flagged for prompt 29 (adjustments by kind). [D92]
- The Extras section is hidden from the Planned sheet; the cost views show
  accepted extras only.
