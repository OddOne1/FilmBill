## Part 2 — assignments: scheduled, then booked
- Migration, company-scoped, soft deletion only:
  - `crew_assignments`: `project_id`, `block_id` (a strip of that project), `party_id` (`is_crew`), `role_item_id` nullable (a labor item), `status` (`scheduled` | `booked` | `declined` | `cancelled`), `day_rate NUMERIC(18,2)` nullable (copied from the effective rate when created, editable, cost data), `status_note`, `booked_on`, `booked_by`, timestamps. Unique active (block, party).
  - `crew_assignment_days`: `assignment_id`, `date` (inside the strip's range), optional `call_time`, `wrap_time` overrides (null = the day's or the strip's default). On creation the assignment gets **every day of the strip that is not `off`**; the user can untick days (someone only on days 3 to 5, or arriving on the travel day).
  - `crew_unavailability`: `party_id`, `start_date`, `end_date`, `reason` (free text), `note`, soft removal. A person's own "not available" periods.
- **Status path:** `scheduled → booked`, `scheduled → declined`, `booked → cancelled`, and `booked → scheduled` (reopen) with a note; every change audited; **booked is marked by hand** (a note field for how it was confirmed, e.g. "by phone"). Bulk: **Mark selected as booked**.
- **Clashes warn and never block.** A person with `scheduled` or `booked` assignments on **overlapping dates** (any project, any strip), or inside an unavailability period, produces a warning list on creation, on change of days and in the UI (which project, which strip, which dates, booked or scheduled). `declined` and `cancelled` assignments do not count. The same assignment never clashes with itself.
- **Not crew:** assigning a party that is not flagged crew is refused with a plain sentence and a one-click "Mark as crew".

Every response that can carry a rate omits it without `budgets.view`.
- **Permissions:** viewing needs `projects.view` and `parties.view` (so staff do not see crew names yet, D111, say so in the response of the staff walk test); changing needs `projects.edit`; unavailability needs `parties.edit`. Missing permission answers 404.

Tests (real Postgres): creation with the default days (strip with an `off` day excluded), unticking days, call and wrap overrides; status paths and the refused ones; bulk booking; clash with scheduled and with booked in another project, none with declined/cancelled, none with itself, the unavailability overlap (state the dates); a non-crew party refused; day outside the strip refused; availability numbers for a three-person range with role and rate filters; staff and permission matrices; isolation rows; no DELETE route. **Mutations, a real FAIL line each:** a cancelled assignment still produces a clash; an assignment day outside the strip accepted; bulk booking books a declined assignment.
