## Part 1 — the pure calculation engine `apps/api/billing/calc/`
Pure Python, no SQLAlchemy or FastAPI imports (enforce with the same fresh-interpreter test as `billing/tax`, plus a second test proving the probe can fail). Inputs and outputs are plain value objects; money is `Decimal` only.
- **Line** (SCOPE §5.3): `id`, `quantity`, `period_value` (default 1), `period_unit` (`hour|half_day|day|week|month|night|flat|piece|km`), `factor` (default 1), `unit_price`, `unit_cost` (optional, default 0), `discount` (`percent` or `amount`, optional), `tax_ref` (opaque string, for grouping only), `kind` (opaque string, for kind-filtered adjustments), `tags` (list of strings). Formula: `net = round2(quantity × period_value × factor × unit_price × (1 − discount%))` or minus the amount; same formula with `unit_cost` for `cost_net`. Round half up to 2 dp, per line.
- **Group**: `id`, `title`, ordered children (lines or groups), **max nesting depth 2** (a third level is a validation error with a readable message), optional adjustments, subtotal.
- **Adjustment** (§5.4): `label`, `type` (`discount|surcharge`), `mode` (`percent|amount`), `base` (`subtotal` | `subtotal_incl_previous` | `kinds: [...]`), attachable to a group or to the document; document adjustments are an ordered list; each rounded to 2 dp.
- **Tax grouping, no tax rates inside the engine's core path:** the engine returns net per `tax_ref` after allocating adjustments pro rata by net (§5.4). A separate optional call `apply_vat(result, rates: dict[str, Decimal])` computes **VAT per tax_ref on the sum of nets** (not per line, §5.6), then gross. Budgets will use the core path only.
- **Output**: per-line `net`, `cost_net`, `margin` (`net − cost_net`) and `margin_percent` (`None` when net is 0); per-group subtotals (price and cost); document totals (price, cost, margin); net per tax_ref; and a warning list (e.g. `mixed_days_in_group`) per §5.4's uniform-days rule: a helper `display_row_for_group(group)` returns the "price/day × days" form only when every line in the group has the same period, otherwise the "1 × total" form plus the warning code.
- **Closed validation, readable errors:** negative quantity allowed only if the line is flagged `allow_negative` (corrections); factor > 0; discount percent within 0–100; unknown enum values rejected with the value named.
- **No expression language.** Quantity and period fields are numbers. Globals (slice A2) will be resolved into numbers **before** the engine is called; the engine never evaluates names.
Golden fixtures, written as data files next to the tests:
  - **S1 quote** (verify to the cent; I computed them independently): Crew group (DoP 1×3×850 = 2,550.00; Gaffer 1×3×550 = 1,650.00; group discount −5 % = −210.00 → 3,990.00); Equipment (Alexa 1×3×1,200 = 3,600.00; lens set 2×3×180 = 1,080.00 → 4,680.00); Studio (1×2×1,400 = 2,800.00); Travel taxi flat 120.00. Subtotal 11,590.00; production fee +8 % on subtotal = 927.20; **net 12,517.20; VAT 20 % 2,503.44; gross 15,020.64**.
  - **S6 rounding**: line 3 × 2.5 d × 333.33 → 2,499.98 (half up), plus seven lines of 1 × 0.333 → 0.33 each, 2.31 in total; net 2,502.29; VAT 20 % 500.46; gross 3,002.75. (Rounding per line, VAT on the sum, per §5.6. If you read S6 differently, say how and why before coding.)
  - Compound adjustment (`subtotal_incl_previous`) and a kind-filtered fee (8 % on `labor` and `equipment_rental` only); mixed tax_refs with a pro rata percent and amount adjustment (nets and VAT per ref sum to the document totals to the cent); cost and margin for S1 with unit costs you choose and state; negative correction line; depth-3 refused; uniform and mixed days rows; zero-net margin.
