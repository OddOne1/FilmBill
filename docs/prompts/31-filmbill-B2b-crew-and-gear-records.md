  - `device_units`: `device_id`, `serial_number` (required, unique among a device's units, case-insensitive), `status` (`active` | `in_repair` | `retired`), `acquired_on`, `retired_on`, `retired_reason` (`destroyed` | `lost` | `sold` | `kept`), `notes`, timestamps. **No hard delete**: a unit that was entered by mistake is retired with a note, never deleted.
