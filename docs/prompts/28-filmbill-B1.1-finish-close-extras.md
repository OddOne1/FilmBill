## Part 3 — closing a budget, with a review
A budget can be **closed** only when it is locked. Closing is the production's end: cost entries and ETCs are then refused until it is reopened.
- `GET /budgets/{id}/close-check` returns the discrepancies, each with ids so the UI can link to the line: lines over their estimate; lines under their estimate that are not marked finished; lines marked finished with an open commitment; **open commitments** in total; unallocated costs; and the budget-level picture in one of three plain states: `within_estimate` (the total forecast is at or below the total estimate, even if single lines are over and others under: money moved between lines), `over_estimate`, or `clean` (no line over, none unexplained). A digest of the result is returned.
- `POST /budgets/{id}/close` requires `budgets.edit`. If the check is `clean` it closes. Otherwise it **requires** `acknowledge: true`, the digest, and a note of at least one sentence, and **refuses, writing nothing, if the digest no longer matches**. It stores `closed_at`, `closed_by`, `close_note` and a snapshot of the discrepancies in the audit event `budget.closed`. `POST /budgets/{id}/reopen` requires a reason, audited, and clears the closed state.
- Screen: a **Close budget** button on a locked budget opens a dialog that lists the discrepancies in plain words, says whether the total stays within the estimate, offers a link to **add an extra charge** (Part 4) when something is over, and has an approve checkbox plus the note. A closed budget says so at the top and offers **Reopen**.
- A closed budget refuses commitments, actuals, ETCs and finish/reopen with a sentence that says reopen it first; extras need the budget **not closed** (reopen first). Record both as decisions for Mathias.
Tests (real Postgres): clean close; close with discrepancies refused without acknowledgement, without note, with a stale digest; allowed with all three; `within_estimate` vs `over_estimate` fixtures (state the numbers); closing an unlocked budget refused; closed refuses cost entries and finish; reopen restores; audit events; isolation; staff walk. **Mutations, a real FAIL line each:** close accepted without acknowledgement when discrepancies exist; a stale digest accepted; a closed budget accepts an actual. ## Part 4 — extras (extra charges agreed after the quote)
An extra is the way the price changes after the lock. It reuses the engine, VAT and tracking by being a **budget line in a reserved "Extras" section** rather than a new calculation path.
- Migration: `budget_sections.is_extras bool default false` (at most one per budget, created on first use, never deletable, never renamable); `budget_lines.extra_status` nullable (`proposed` | `accepted` | `declined`), plus `extra_decided_on` date and `extra_note` text. A line in the Extras section must have a status; a line elsewhere must not. Lines in the Extras section carry price and cost like any line (catalog items allowed) and a note (what the customer was told).
- **Calculation:** only `accepted` extras enter the engine document that produces the budget totals, VAT and tracking baseline; `proposed` and `declined` extras are listed but excluded from every total. The calculated view returns **two totals**: `baseline` (everything except extras, equal to the locked quote) and `with_extras`, and each extra's own price, cost and margin. The top sheet shows both.
- **Lock behaviour:** the Extras section stays editable on a **locked** budget (propose, edit, accept, decline); every other estimate edit is still refused. Accepting sets `extra_decided_on`; changing an accepted extra's price after acceptance is allowed but audited with before/after. Nothing here is hard-deleted: a withdrawn extra is `declined` with a note.
- **Costs against extras:** commitments and actuals may be booked on an accepted extra's line like on any line; their forecast uses the extra's estimate cost.
- Duplicating a budget copies the baseline only: no extras, no ETCs, no closed state. CSV export includes extras with a status column; CSV import refuses rows in the Extras section ("extras are managed on the Extras tab").
- Audit: `budget_extra.proposed|updated|accepted|declined`. Permissions: `budgets.view` / `budgets.edit`, no new keys; staff see none.
- Screen: an **Extras** tab on the budget (list with status chips, add, edit, accept, decline with note), and on the top sheet a row "Extras accepted" and the two totals. Without `budgets.edit` the buttons are absent and a sentence says why.
Tests (real Postgres): the S1 example budget locked, then an extra proposed at price 500.00 / cost 300.00: totals unchanged; accepted: with-extras price is the baseline plus 500.00 plus VAT handling exactly as the engine does for a normal line (state every number, VAT included); declined: excluded again; ordinary line edit on the locked budget refused while the extra edit is allowed; costs booked on the extra; duplicate budget copies none; CSV; isolation; staff walk. **Mutations, a real FAIL line each:** a declined extra counted in `with_extras`; a proposed extra counted; an ordinary line editable on a locked budget because it sits next to the Extras section.
